# Analytics

The Analytics tab shows four read-only charts that give you a quick overview of your finances. Each chart loads independently, so if one fails to load you'll see a "couldn't load" message for that chart while the others still work.

## Spending breakdown

This chart shows how your spending is distributed across categories. It answers the question "Where does my money go?" Look at the relative sizes of the slices (or bars) to see which categories take up the most of your spending.

## Budget vs actual

This chart compares your budgeted amounts to what you actually spent. It answers the question "Am I staying within my budget?" For each category or period, you'll see two values: the budgeted amount and the actual amount. If the actual amount is higher than the budgeted amount, you're over budget.

## Spending trend

This chart shows how your spending changes over time. It answers the question "Is my spending going up or down?" Look at the direction of the line or bars: upward means spending is increasing, downward means it's decreasing.

## Savings rate

This chart shows the percentage of your income that you're saving over time. It answers the question "How much am I saving?" Look at the line: higher values mean you're saving a larger share of your income.

## Note about read-only charts

All four charts are read-only – you can't edit the data from the chart itself. The same chart component is used inside chat conversations, so a chart you ask for in chat looks exactly the same here.
