# Dashboard

The Dashboard is the home screen of the app. It shows a configurable set of widgets and a period selector.

## Header

The header shows the current date, the app title, and actions for notifications and settings.

## Notification permission banner

If you haven't granted notification permission, you'll see a banner asking you to enable it. Enabling notifications lets the app send you alerts about your budget, goals, and other important updates.

## On budget total

At the top of the widget area, you'll see the "On budget" total. This is the total amount you've spent within your budget categories for the selected period.

## Empty state

If this is a brand-new install, you'll see a "Let's get started" message. It links to the setup flow where you can connect your accounts and start tracking your finances.

## Widgets

### Goals

The Goals widget shows your savings goals, your FIRE (Financial Independence, Retire Early) progress, and your expense coverage ratio.

### Categories Watchlist

This widget displays the categories you've chosen to watch for the current budget period. You can enter group edit mode to add or remove categories.

### Balance trend

The Balance trend widget shows a 30-day history line for your total balance, on-budget balance, portfolio, and vehicles. It also shows the change compared to 30 days ago. Some scopes may not have a data source yet, in which case they'll be marked as unavailable.

### Latest Transactions

This widget lists your five most recent transactions. Clicking any transaction takes you to the Transactions tab.

### Expenses Structure

The Expenses Structure widget shows a pie chart of your spending by category for the selected period.

### Cash Flow

The Cash Flow widget is currently a placeholder. It will show your income and expenses over time in a future update.

### Vehicle costs

This widget shows the total cost of your vehicles for the period, the number of vehicles you have, and the cost per kilometer. If you have no vehicles, it will show a "no vehicles" state. If vehicle data is unavailable, it will show an "unavailable" state.

### Net Worth

The Net Worth widget shows your current total net worth, a history line, and the start, now, and growth figures. You can use the Include menu to choose whether to include loans, vehicles, and rental properties in the calculation.

## Customize mode

To customize which widgets appear on your Dashboard, click the Customize button in the bottom bar. This enters customize mode. In this mode, you can:

- Remove a widget by clicking the × chip on its card.
- Add a widget back by selecting it from the "Add widgets" list.

When you're done, click Done to save your changes, or Cancel to discard them. Your widget choices are remembered the next time you visit the Dashboard.

## Bottom bar

The bottom bar contains the Customize button and the period control. Use the left and right arrows to move to the previous or next period. Tap the period label to open a picker sheet that shows the last 12 months, grouped by year.
