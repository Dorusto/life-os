---
title: Dashboard
description: See your portfolio at a glance — value, returns, allocation and top movers.
---

The **Dashboard** is the main overview page for your investment portfolio. It shows your current
portfolio value, performance over a chosen period, a historical value chart, an allocation breakdown,
and a list of top movers.

## Period selector

At the top of the page you’ll find a row of period buttons: **1M**, **3M**, **6M**, **1Y**, **2Y**,
**5Y**, and **All**.  Changing the period updates both the summary figures (statement panel) and the
value chart so that every metric reflects the same time span.

## Statement panel

The big statement card at the top is the most prominent piece of data on the screen.  It shows:

- **Portfolio value** — the total market value of all holdings in EUR.
- **Change** — the absolute and percentage change in value over the selected period, displayed with
  an up or down arrow.
- **Cost basis** — the total amount of money you have put into the portfolio (sum of purchase costs).
- **Unrealized gain** — the profit (or loss) you would realise if you sold everything today, shown
  both in EUR and as a percentage of cost basis.
- **XIRR (money‑weighted)** — the internal rate of return that accounts for the timing of your
  deposits and withdrawals.
- **TWR (time‑weighted)** — the time‑weighted rate of return, which removes the effect of cash flows
  and lets you compare your portfolio’s investment performance to a benchmark.
- **Benchmark return** — the return of a market index (set in Settings) over the same period.
- **Portfolio vs benchmark** — the difference between your TWR and the benchmark return, indicating
  whether you are outperforming or underperforming the market.  This row also shows the start date
  of the period.

## Portfolio value chart

Below the statement panel you’ll find a line chart of the portfolio’s daily value in EUR over the
selected period.  A note next to the chart title reminds you that values are *daily*.

Hover anywhere on the chart to see a cross‑hair and a tooltip that shows the exact date and
portfolio value for that point in time.

## Allocation card

The allocation card sits next to the value chart and shows how your money is distributed across
different slices, based on the view you choose:

- **Asset type** (stock, ETF, crypto, bond, fund, other)
- **Holding** (each individual security)
- **Currency**

A **donut chart** in the centre displays the same proportions visually, with the total portfolio
value in the middle of the ring.  Below the donut a **bar list** gives each slice’s exact EUR
amount and percentage.

Hovering over a donut segment or its corresponding legend row highlights that piece by making its
stroke slightly thicker.

## Top movers

The **top movers** card lists the securities with the largest unrealised return (gain or loss) in
the current period — the positions that are moving your portfolio’s performance most.

Each row shows the ticker, the security name, and the change in EUR and percentage.

If you have no priced positions yet (for example, right after importing data but before a market
prices are available), the card shows a simple “No priced positions to rank yet.” message.

## When there are no holdings

If your portfolio has no holdings at all, you will see an **empty state** screen instead of the
regular Dashboard.  It explains that you need to add a security and a transaction (or import an XTB
report) and provides a button linking to the **Transactions** page where you can get started.
