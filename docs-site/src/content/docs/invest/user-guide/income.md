---
title: Income
description: View a summary of dividend payments received, with breakdowns by year and by holding.
---

The **Income** page shows all dividend payments your portfolio has produced. It uses the same data you record through manual transactions or via an XTB import.

## Summary tiles

Four tiles at the top give you a quick overview:

- **Total received** – the combined dividend income across all holdings, converted to EUR.
- **Payments** – the total number of dividend events in the system.
- **Years covered** – how many distinct years are represented in the data.
- **Latest year** – the total dividend amount for the most recent year available.

## Breakdowns

Two bar charts let you explore where the income is coming from.

### By year

Each bar represents a calendar year and its total dividend amount, expressed as a percentage of the overall total. The length of the bar shows the relative size.

### By holding

Each bar corresponds to a single security (ticker) and shows its total dividend contribution, also as a percentage of the overall total.

## Dividend history table

Below the charts is a detailed table with every dividend payment recorded. The columns are:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| **Date** | The date the dividend was received, displayed as day–month–year. |
| **Security** | The ticker symbol and the full name of the holding (if available). |
| **Native** | The amount in the original currency of the payment. |
| **EUR** | The amount converted to euros. |

## Empty state

If no dividends have been recorded yet, the page displays a message:

> **No dividends recorded**  
> Dividends appear here once they're added as transactions — manually or via an XTB import.

Once you start recording dividend transactions (either by hand or by importing a brokerage statement), the tiles, breakdowns, and table populate automatically.
