Majordom Invest is a web‑based portfolio tracker that organizes your investments across a handful of focused screens. You can reach any screen from the left‑hand navigation rail on desktop, or from the top bar and a slide‑over drawer on mobile. This section describes each screen so you can quickly find your way around.

| Screen | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| [Dashboard](/invest/user-guide/dashboard) | Portfolio summary: value, returns, allocation chart, top movers, and a line chart of daily value. |
| [Holdings](/invest/user-guide/holdings) | Open positions with average cost, market value, unrealized gain, and allocation weight. |
| [Transactions](/invest/user-guide/transactions) | Every buy, sell, dividend, and fee, listed newest first; add or delete transactions. |
| [Income](/invest/user-guide/income) | Dividend payments received over time, broken down by year and by holding. |
| [Rebalancing](/invest/user-guide/rebalancing) | Compare current allocation to target percentages and see suggested trades to close gaps. |
| [Goals](/invest/user-guide/goals) | Set target amounts and dates, and track whether your portfolio is on pace to meet them. |
| [Settings](/invest/user-guide/settings) | Benchmark ticker, assumed annual return, and Twelve Data API key (write‑only). |

## Navigation

On desktop the app’s main navigation is a persistent left‑hand rail. On mobile the rail slides in as a drawer that you open from the top‑bar hamburger icon. A theme toggle (light/dark) lives in the footer of the rail on all screen sizes.

## Where to enter data

The **Dashboard** is the summary page—it gives you a quick overview of your portfolio value, returns, allocation, and top movers. All the other screens are where you enter, edit, or correct your data. Use **Transactions** to record buys, sells, dividends, and fees. Use **Holdings** to review open positions. **Income** shows your dividend history. **Rebalancing** helps you keep your allocation in line with your targets. **Goals** lets you set future targets and track progress. **Settings** holds a few global preferences.
