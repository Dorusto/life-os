The Chat tab is Majordom Finance's primary interface. You ask in plain language, and Majordom answers with text, charts, or a card asking you to confirm an action.

## Header

Beside the "Majordom" title sit the shared notification bell and settings gear, plus an overflow menu with two options: **Clear chat history** and **How to use Majordom**, which opens a help sheet with example questions — worth a look before your first conversation instead of guessing what to ask.

## Starting a conversation

On an empty conversation, a few starter suggestions are shown as tap-to-send chips, e.g. "How much did I spend this month?", "Am I on budget?", "What are my biggest expenses?".

## The input bar

- The **+** button opens a media menu: **Take photo**, **Choose from gallery**, or **Upload CSV**.
- Type in the text area; **Enter** sends the message, **Shift+Enter** adds a newline instead.
- The up/down arrow keys step back and forth through your previously sent messages.

## What Majordom can reply with

- **Plain text answers**, formatted with markdown.
- **Charts and transaction lists** — read-only, and they stay in the conversation history after a refresh.
- **Confirmation cards** for any action that would change your data: proposals, budget rebalance, account transfer, balance adjustment, close account, transfer conversion, category actions, budget copy, reached goals, goal proposals, vehicle log actions, vehicle reminders, vehicle status, and notification time. Every field on a card is editable before you confirm.
- **Clarification cards**, when Majordom needs you to pick one of a few options before it can continue.
- **Receipt cards**, for reviewing data extracted from a photo — you can switch the card between fuel and grocery before saving.
- **CSV import cards** and **Fuelio import cards**, previewing what will be imported before you confirm.

Once you act on a card (confirm or cancel), it's replaced by a short status line — "Cancelled.", "Saved…" — and the exchange stays in your chat history.

## First run

If you haven't set up any account balances yet, the first message you'll see is a setup card asking you to enter your real starting balances, followed by a confirmation once they're saved.

## Learning what to ask

Rather than repeat every example here, open **How to use Majordom** from the overflow menu — the in-app help sheet lists example questions and stays up to date with what the app can actually do.
