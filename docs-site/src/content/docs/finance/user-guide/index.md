Majordom Finance is organized around a small number of tabs. This section walks through each one, so you can quickly find the screen you need.

| Screen | What it does | Link |
|--------|--------------|------|
| Dashboard | Gives you a high‑level view of your finances at a glance. | [Dashboard](./dashboard) |
| Accounts | Manage your bank accounts, credit cards, and other financial accounts. | [Accounts](./accounts) |
| Transactions | See and edit individual transactions. | [Transactions](./transactions) |
| Analytics | Explore charts and reports about your spending and income. | [Analytics](./analytics) |
| Chat | The primary way to interact with Majordom — ask questions, give commands, and get things done. | [Chat](./chat) |
| Settings | Configure your preferences, notifications, and other app options. | [Settings](./settings) |
| Import | Bring in data from a CSV file. | [Import](./import) |

## Navigation

The app uses a bottom tab bar to switch between the main screens. At the top you’ll find header actions, such as notifications and settings. Settings itself uses a sub‑page pattern: you tap a menu item and a new page opens with a back button to return.

## Chat is the primary way to do things

The Chat tab is where you can ask Majordom to perform actions, such as creating a transaction or generating a report. The other tabs are for looking at and correcting what Majordom has done — for example, reviewing transactions, checking your accounts, or adjusting settings.
