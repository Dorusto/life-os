---
title: Accounts
description: Find and manage your Actual Budget accounts, group them, link vehicles, and view account details.
---

The Accounts tab lists every Actual Budget account, groups them, and lets you drill down into a single account.

## Account list

The top of the page shows the total across all accounts. Below that, accounts are grouped into three sections:

- **Vehicles** – accounts linked to a vehicle profile.
- **On budget** – accounts that are part of your budget.
- **Off budget** – accounts that are not part of your budget.

Each account row shows an icon based on the account type, the account name, and its current balance. Tapping a row opens the account detail page.

## Vehicles section

The Vehicles section shows a subtotal of all vehicle‑linked accounts. It includes an **Add vehicle** button.

Each vehicle row shows the make, model, and year when the account is linked to a vehicle profile. If the account is not linked, the row shows **Not linked to a vehicle profile — tap to link**.

### Linking a vehicle

Tapping a vehicle row opens the **LinkVehicleSheet**. This sheet lists vehicle profiles that are not yet linked to any account. You can select one to link it to the current account.

If there are no unlinked vehicles, the sheet shows a message that no vehicles are available to link. In that case, you can use the **Add vehicle** button to create a new vehicle profile first.

## Account detail page

Tapping an account row opens the account detail page.

### Balance header

The top of the page shows the account’s current balance.

### Details tab

The Details tab shows:

- Whether the account is **On budget** or **Off budget**.
- The account’s **category** and **account type**.

You can edit the account type inline. When you tap the type, a list of available types appears. Choose one to update the account.

### Transactions tab

The Transactions tab shows that account’s transactions, grouped by month. Each month shows a net total for that month.

If the account has no transactions, the tab shows an empty state.

## Data source

Account data comes from Actual Budget. You cannot edit the account name, balance, or transactions here. The only thing you can change is the account type, as described above.
