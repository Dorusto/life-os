title: Statistics
description: View detailed statistics for a vehicle — fuel consumption, costs, distance, and a cost-categories breakdown.
---

The **Statistics** page gives you a detailed breakdown of your vehicle’s fuel economy, running costs, and driving habits. Use the vehicle switcher at the top to pick the vehicle you’d like to analyse — all figures update automatically.

## Tabs overview

Three switchable views cover different aspects:

- **Fill‑ups** – consumption and fuel totals.  
- **Costs** – all expense figures, from total bills to cost per kilometre.  
- **Distance** – distance driven over time.

A segmented control lets you move between them.

## Fill‑ups tab

The Fill‑ups view presents:

- **Fill‑up count** – how many times you’ve refuelled.  
- **Average consumption** – litres per 100 km.  
- **Total fuel** – total litres purchased.  
- **Total fuel cost** – all‑time fuel spend.

## Costs tab

The Costs view shows a range of expense metrics:

- **Total costs** – all‑time running costs (including fuel unless you uncheck below).  
- **This year** / **This month** – costs for the current calendar year and month.  
- **Previous year** / **Previous month** – costs for the preceding year and month.  

Below the summary you’ll find comparison cards:

- **Lowest bill** – the smallest single bill.  
- **Highest bill** – the largest single bill.  
- **Best price/litre** – the cheapest fuel price you’ve recorded.  
- **Worst price/litre** – the most expensive fuel price you’ve recorded.  
- **Average cost per km** – total cost (including fuel) divided by distance.  
- **Average cost per day** – total cost divided by the number of days since the first entry.

## Distance tab

The Distance view provides:

- **Total distance** – all‑time odometer difference.  
- **This year** / **This month** – distance for the current period.  
- **Average per month** – mean monthly distance.  
- **Average per day** – mean daily distance.

## Cost‑categories chart

Below the three tabs you’ll find a chart that breaks down your costs by category (fuel, maintenance, insurance, etc.). Use the **Include fuel** checkbox to toggle fuel costs on or off — unchecking it lets you see non‑fuel expenses more clearly.

The chart is generated from the same data used elsewhere on the page. If no figures are available yet (for example, right after creating a vehicle), the chart and most numbers will show a dash (`—`) instead of a value.

## No data yet

If you haven’t added any log entries for a vehicle, the page will display a **No statistics yet.** message and the per‑tab content area will be empty. Start adding fuel fill‑ups, costs or odometer readings from the vehicle’s log to populate the statistics.
